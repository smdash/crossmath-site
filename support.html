Common questions

How do I play?

Every row and column is a small sum. Fill the empty squares with the tiles from your rack so that every line is true. Where two lines cross they share a number, so one tile can settle two sums. × and ÷ are worked out before + and −, the way you learned at school. Drag a tile onto the board, or tap a square and then a tile. Drag a placed tile off the board to send it back.

Is there always exactly one answer?

Yes. Every board is checked when it is made: it has a single solution, and that solution can be reached by reasoning, without trial and error. If you ever find a board with two valid answers, please email us the level and a screenshot.

Is Cross Math free?

Yes. Every level and the daily puzzle are free. A banner sits at the top of the board while you play, and a short ad can appear after you finish a puzzle or when you leave one before finishing it.

How do hints work?

You get three free hints every day. A hint fills the square that is closest to being decided. When you run out, you can watch a short video for three more. If ads can't load, for example with no connection, hints are free.

How does the daily puzzle work?

There is one daily puzzle per day, the same for every player. It gets harder through the week: easy on Monday, expert on Saturday. Solve it on the day to keep your streak; your calendar is under Statistics.

Do my stats and streak sync between devices?

Not yet. Cross Math has no account and keeps your stats, streak and unfinished puzzles on the device itself. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I stop personalised ads?

On your device open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Cross Math off. Ads still appear, but are no longer personalised. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you can also change your consent from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

Can I hide the timer or the red mistakes?

Yes, in the app's Settings. Your times are still recorded when the timer is hidden.

Reporting a problem

The best report says what you tapped, what happened, and what you expected. Your device model and iOS version help too. Email programmingdash@gmail.com.
