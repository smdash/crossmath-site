Privacy Policy

Cross Math is a number puzzle app. This page says exactly what it stores on your device, what leaves your device, and what it never collects.

Last updated 16 November 2026 · Applies to Cross Math for iPhone and iPad

The short version. There is no account and no sign-in. Your puzzles, stats and streak stay on your device and are never uploaded. The only thing that goes online is advertising, described in full below. There is no analytics SDK, and your data is never sold or shared by us.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's own storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no access to it.

WhatWhy it exists
Unfinished puzzlesThe tiles you have placed, your time, mistakes and hints, so you can continue where you left off.
Statistics and streakPuzzles solved and best times per level, and the days you solved the daily puzzle, to show your stats and streak.
HintsHow many hints you have left and when they were last topped up.
SettingsShow mistakes, timer, haptics, appearance, whether you have seen the tutorial, and a counter used to space out ads when you leave a puzzle.

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no iCloud sync at present, so your stats belong to the device they were made on.

Permissions the app asks for

Cross Math does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notification access. The only permission it asks about is Tracking (the "Allow Cross Math to track" prompt), which exists for advertising and is explained next. You can decline and the whole app keeps working.

Advertising

Cross Math is free and funded by ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads come in three forms only:

  • a banner at the top of the screen while you play;
  • a short full-screen ad after you finish a puzzle, and sometimes when you leave a puzzle before finishing it;
  • an optional video you can choose to watch for three extra hints.

No ad covers the board, and no full-screen ad appears while you are solving.

To select and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them.
  • Approximate location at country or region level, inferred from your IP address. The app never requests location permission.
  • Diagnostic data about the performance of the advertising SDK.

Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from partner apps. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first asks for your consent through Google's consent form, which you can change later from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the app.

To stop personalised ads anywhere: on your device open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Cross Math off.

What the app does not collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or passwords.
  • No analytics or tracking tools other than the advertising SDK described above.
  • Your puzzles, stats and settings are never uploaded to any server.
  • We never sell or share your data, and you will never receive marketing messages.

Children

Cross Math is a general-audience app, not directed at children, and is rated 4+ on the App Store. Its advertising configuration does not treat it as child-directed.

Changes

If the app's data behaviour changes, this page is updated in the same release and the date above changes with it.
