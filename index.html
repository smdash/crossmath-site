Make every line true.

Cross Math is a crossword made of small sums. Rows and columns cross on shared numbers; place the tiles from your rack so that every one of them adds up.

7 + 5 = 12  ·  2 + 3 × 4 = 14

What makes it different

  • No guessing, ever. Every board is checked before you see it: it has exactly one answer, and you can reach it by reasoning alone.
  • Levels that mean something. Easy uses + and − on a gentle board; Medium adds ×; Hard brings all four signs; Expert has long chains and few clues.
  • A daily puzzle that is the same for everyone and climbs through the week: easy on Monday, expert on Saturday. Keep your streak going.
  • Quick to play. Drag a tile straight onto the board, or tap a square and then a tile. Lines turn green when they hold. Undo anything.
  • Hints that help. A hint fills the square that is closest to being decided, so it teaches the next step instead of spoiling the board.
  • Calm by design. A paper-and-ink look, light and dark mode, no coins, no energy, no timers you can't hide.

Free, with ads. A banner sits at the top of the board while you play, and a short ad can appear after you finish a puzzle or when you leave one. Nothing covers the board while you are solving. Three hints are free every day; a short video adds more. Read the privacy policy.
